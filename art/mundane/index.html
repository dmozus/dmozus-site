I Wanted To Do Mundane Things With You

2025 · analog collage

Inspired by a late-night phone call with a friend who said, “I wanted to do the boring things with him.”

The image is from a 1958 magazine and the words come from modern ones. Who knew it would be so hard to find the word “things.” This one hurt my heart, and maybe it will hurt yours too.

Analog collage, cut and pasted by hand from vintage ephemera.
